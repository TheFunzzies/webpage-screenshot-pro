Webpage Screenshot Pro

A Chrome extension that saves a whole web page, the visible area or a region you draw as a small PDF with real, searchable text, or as a JPEG or PNG image.

  • Full pages, top to bottom, including lazy-loaded images
  • Compression levels that shrink photo-heavy pages by up to 95%
  • Hides chat bubbles and cookie banners; handles pinned headers and sidebars
  • Save all open tabs at once, or copy a page to the clipboard
  • Everything happens on your computer: no accounts, no uploads, no tracking
